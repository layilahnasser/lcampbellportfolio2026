Design system

CLOSED · reasons visible

A teal and gold palette with a serif and sans pairing, in light and dark mode.

Why teal. Calm, focus and trust before an interview.

Why gold. Achievement and prestige, saved for progress moments.

Why a serif plus a sans. Polish where it is seen, legibility where it is used.
